Privacy Policy

Effective: 6 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ORION Publisher processes personal data. ORION Publisher is operated by Valentijn Jacobs in the Netherlands, who is the controller for the processing described here. Questions and privacy requests can be sent to vbjtechservices@gmail.com.

1. Scope

This policy applies to the ORION Publisher information website and to publishing workflows that connect creator-controlled accounts to supported social media platforms. A connected platform also processes data under its own privacy policy.

2. Data we process

We obtain data directly from you, from connected platforms after you authorize access, and from the operation of the service.

Depending on the features used, ORION Publisher may process:

  • Account and authorization data: platform account identifiers, display information returned by an authorized platform, requested permissions, access tokens, refresh tokens, and token-expiry information.
  • Publication content: videos, captions, hashtags, selected destination accounts, privacy choices, and scheduling instructions.
  • Publication records: approval state, scheduled and published times, platform publication identifiers, delivery status, and error information.
  • Operational data: security events, audit records, service logs, and diagnostic information needed to maintain reliable delivery.
  • Communications: information included when you contact us for support or make a privacy request.

We do not ask you to provide your social media password. Authorization is completed on the platform's own consent screen.

3. Purposes and legal bases

We process data to:

  • connect and identify accounts that you authorize;
  • review, schedule, publish, and reconcile content according to approved instructions;
  • refresh or revoke platform authorization where supported;
  • secure, troubleshoot, audit, and improve the service;
  • comply with legal duties and enforce the Terms of Service; and
  • respond to questions and data-rights requests.
  • Contract: to connect an authorized account, follow approved publishing instructions, provide support, and administer the service.
  • Consent: where you grant optional platform permissions or another processing activity specifically requests consent. You may withdraw consent without affecting earlier lawful processing.
  • Legitimate interests: to secure the service, prevent misuse, diagnose failures, maintain auditability, and improve reliable delivery, balanced against your rights.
  • Legal obligations: to retain or disclose information where applicable law requires it.

4. Required data and automated decisions

Account identifiers, authorization tokens, publishing instructions, and the content to be published are required to provide the connected publishing workflow. Without them, ORION Publisher cannot connect the account or complete the requested publication. ORION Publisher does not use personal data for solely automated decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects.

5. How data is shared

We do not sell personal data. Data is shared only where needed to operate the service, including with:

  • Connected social platforms, such as TikTok, when you authorize an account or approve a publication;
  • Infrastructure providers, which may include GitHub Pages for this public website, Supabase for publication metadata, and Discord for restricted operational review and notifications;
  • Authorities or professional advisers, when disclosure is legally required or reasonably necessary to protect rights and security.

The public information website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process standard connection information, such as IP addresses and request logs, under its own privacy terms. This website does not intentionally use advertising trackers or analytics cookies.

6. Storage and security

ORION Publisher uses access controls, encrypted network connections, restricted secret storage, and operational logging designed to protect data. Client secrets and platform tokens are kept outside public source code. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

7. Retention

Authorization tokens are retained while an account remains connected and are removed or invalidated after disconnection, expiry, revocation, or a verified deletion request, unless limited retention is necessary for security or legal reasons. Unpublished content and scheduling data are retained while needed for the requested workflow. Publication, security, and audit records are retained according to their operational value, the need to reconcile deliveries and investigate incidents, applicable limitation periods, and legal obligations. Data that is no longer required under those criteria is deleted or anonymized where reasonably possible.

8. International processing

Some connected platforms and infrastructure providers may process data outside the European Economic Area. Where required, we rely on an applicable legal transfer mechanism and provider safeguards.

9. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or objection to processing of your personal data. You may withdraw consent where processing relies on consent. You may also revoke platform access from the connected platform's settings.

To exercise these rights, email vbjtechservices@gmail.com. We may need to verify your identity before completing a request. We aim to respond without undue delay and normally within one month, subject to applicable law. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority or your local supervisory authority.

10. Disconnection and deletion

You can revoke access in the connected platform's app-permission settings. To request deletion of data held by ORION Publisher, follow the public disconnect and data-deletion process. Deletion from ORION Publisher does not automatically remove content already published on a third-party platform.

11. Cookies and website logs

This information website does not intentionally set advertising or analytics cookies. GitHub Pages may process ordinary request and security logs as the hosting provider. If tracking or non-essential cookies are added later, this policy and any required consent controls will be updated first.

12. Children

ORION Publisher is intended for creators and authorized account managers and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children through this website.

13. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the service, providers, or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top identifies the current version. Material changes will be communicated where reasonably possible.
