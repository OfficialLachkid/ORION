Creator publishing operations

Approved content, delivered on schedule.

ORION Publisher manages, schedules, and publishes approved short-form videos to creator-controlled social media accounts. Creators retain control of account authorization, content, and publishing choices.

Operated by Valentijn Jacobs in the Netherlands

Built around control

A deliberate publishing workflow

ORION Publisher separates content preparation, human approval, scheduling, and platform delivery so that each stage remains visible and auditable.

Creator authorization

Platform access is granted through the platform's official authorization flow and can be revoked by the account owner.

Approval before delivery

Content is reviewed and approved before it becomes eligible for a scheduled publication.

Account-level settings

Publishing choices, including captions and privacy options, are applied per connected creator account.

Operational records

Delivery state and platform responses are retained to support reconciliation, troubleshooting, and safe retries.

How it works

From approved video to platform delivery

  1. Connect an accountThe creator authorizes ORION Publisher using the platform's official login and consent process.
  2. Review the publicationThe creator or operator confirms the content, caption, account, and available privacy settings.
  3. Choose the scheduleAn approved publication is assigned a delivery time in the ORION schedule.
  4. Publish and reconcileORION sends the approved content through the platform API and records the resulting delivery status.
