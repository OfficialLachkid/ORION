Privacy controls

Disconnect and delete your data

Last updated: 6 October 2026

You can disconnect a social account from ORION Publisher and request deletion of personal data associated with that connection. This page describes the current request-based process.

1. Revoke platform access

You may revoke ORION Publisher's authorization from the connected platform's account or app-permission settings. Revocation stops future API access but does not automatically remove records already held by ORION Publisher. To request deletion of those records, complete step 2.

2. Send a deletion request

Email vbjtechservices@gmail.com with the subject ORION Publisher data deletion. Include:

  • the connected platform and account handle or display name;
  • the email address through which we can respond; and
  • whether you want to delete all associated data or only specified records.

Never send your platform password, access token, refresh token, or client secret.

3. Verification and completion

We will acknowledge the request and may ask for limited information needed to confirm that you control the account. Once verified, we will revoke active authorization where supported and delete or irreversibly anonymize covered data, except information that must be retained for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or dispute-resolution purposes. We aim to complete valid requests without undue delay and normally within one month, subject to applicable law.

What the request covers

  • stored access and refresh tokens for the disconnected account;
  • connected-account profile data received from the platform;
  • unpublished scheduled publications associated only with that account;
  • content supplied only for the deleted publishing workflow; and
  • publication and operational records that no longer have to be retained.

Content already published

Deleting ORION Publisher data does not automatically delete a post that has already been published on a third-party platform. Delete that post through the platform itself. The platform processes its own copy of account and publication data under its privacy policy.

Questions or complaints

For privacy questions, email vbjtechservices@gmail.com. You may also lodge a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority or another competent supervisory authority.
